## Crash and outfit-switch fixes

- Fixed a crash that could happen right after switching presets while several transmogged pieces were being removed
- Fixed real items vanishing a moment after switching to a preset with every slot unticked, when the real items were the same items the swap builds on
- Fixed a real item staying on screen after you unequipped it, when the mod had put it back earlier (a preset with every slot unticked, Enabled turned off, or Clear)
- Fixed transmogged earrings and rings ending up on one side, or swapping sides, once the game took off or re-seated the real pair, and a left-hand ring variant never receiving its transmog mesh
- Fixed only one earring or ring coming back when a preset with every slot unticked put the real pair on again
- Made the mod's game-code detection more tolerant of game updates
